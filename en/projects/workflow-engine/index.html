PROJECT CASE STUDY

Workflow Engine

Configurable DAGs, decoupled task management and asynchronous Webhook delivery.

Jul 2024 — Present

40% faster business onboarding

  • DAG
  • Task management
  • Webhook
  • Outbox

Background

Workflow instances and task management were coupled, production work was difficult to operate and measure, and service restarts could interrupt the consistency of local changes and event delivery.

Architecture

  1. Configuration & forms

    Describe business inputs and production workflows through node configuration and dynamic forms.

  2. Workflows & tasks

    Advance DAG execution while task management handles human work, delivery statistics and monitoring.

  3. Events & callbacks

    Deliver Webhooks asynchronously with per-source concurrency control and idempotent processing.

  4. Reliable handoff

    Combine Outbox and graceful shutdown to make local changes and event delivery recoverable.

Key decisions

Configuration-driven business logic

Abstract reusable components and node capabilities, with dynamic forms to reduce repeated integration work.

Separate workflow and task responsibilities

Give human work settlement, production output statistics and end-to-end monitoring clear boundaries.

Asynchronous callbacks and consistent delivery

Decouple Webhooks from workflow components; use idempotency, Outbox and graceful shutdown for reliable long-running collaboration.

Outcomes

  • Reduced new-business integration time by 40%.
  • Supported 2M+ monthly workflow instances across 40+ business lines.
  • Reduced transaction loss to 0.01% through an eventual-consistency approach.
